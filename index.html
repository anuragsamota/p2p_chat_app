⚠️ Mobile Browser Security Restriction

Mobile browsers (Chrome on Android & Safari on iOS) strictly block camera and microphone access over unencrypted HTTP (http://...).

To enable calling on your mobile phone:
Option 1 (Recommended): Use HTTPS

Deploy or open the app on an HTTPS URL (e.g. your GitHub Pages deployment). Camera & mic will work immediately on all phones.

Option 2 (Local LAN Wi-Fi on Android Chrome):
  1. Open a new tab in Chrome: chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
  2. Paste this origin URL into the box:

3. Select Enabled from dropdown and tap Relaunch.
